3. Manual Verification of the Steady-State Governing Equation

For one-dimensional conduction with uniform internal heat generation and constant thermal conductivity:

d²T/dx² = -q’’’/k

The boundary conditions are:

At x = 0:

dT/dx = 0

At x = L:

-k(dT/dx) = h(T(L) - T_inf)

11. Recommended Improvements

Improvement 1 – Maintain Consistent Numerical Parameters

The same time step, material properties, and numerical formulation should be used throughout the report.

Results from simulations using different time steps must not be combined without identifying their respective assumptions.

Improvement 2 – Separate Heating and Convergence Times

The report must distinguish:

* Time required to reach the 100°C volume-average target.
* Time at which the heater is switched off.
* Recovery time after heater shutoff.
* Total time required to approach the original steady-state profile.

These represent different physical events.

Improvement 3 – Explain the Heater Boundary Condition

When the external heater is active, the left boundary is no longer adiabatic.

The boundary condition becomes a prescribed inward heat flux.

After the heater is switched off, the original insulated boundary condition is restored.

Improvement 4 – Improve Numerical Reproducibility

Provide the complete Python program, including the heater shutoff event, convergence calculations, temperature histories, and energy-balance verification.

Improvement 5 – Support Engineering Claims

Material-property assumptions, thermal stress limitations, and the selection of heater shutoff time must be supported by appropriate calculations or references.
